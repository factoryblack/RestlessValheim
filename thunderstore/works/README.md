# RestlessWorks

A work-order board for the kilns, smelters and other production machines around your workshop.

Place the board from the hammer, under Furniture, at a workbench. Use it to leave instructions. The machines nearby do the work. The food preparation table stays the kitchen, and the stone oven stays with cooking.

Needs **RestlessCore**, plus BepInEx and Jötunn. Everyone on the server should be on the same minor version.

The workshop window is not in this package yet. The board still keeps the orders, and it will load and collect once they are placed.

Not in the Restless Valheim pack yet.
